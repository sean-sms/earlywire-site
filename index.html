EarlyWire

Your morning, ready before you are.

Coming to iPhone, iPad and Mac

EarlyWire gets your mornings ready overnight, on your own device: the day's best sports and news videos, new music from your favorite artists, and written briefs about the companies, topics or hobbies you follow, to read or to listen to.

Videos and music, one tap

Sports highlights, top news stories and new songs, picked by rules you can change. Tap Play all and they play in the YouTube app, ad-free if you have Premium.

Written briefs

Follow anything: clients, competitors, a topic, a recipe box. Each item shows the article's own opening sentence, with a link to read more. Make as many briefs as you like.

Listen

Hear your brief read aloud, with "More" and "Next" from your headphones, the lock screen or your voice. Start it with Siri or a Lock Screen control.

Private by design

Everything is worked out on your device. No account, no server, no ads and no tracking. Add your own Claude key if you want better summaries.

Widgets for the Home and Lock Screens show what's ready and when the next briefs build.
