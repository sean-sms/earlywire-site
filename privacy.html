Privacy policy

EarlyWire, by Sean Scott. Last updated October 9, 2026.

The short version

EarlyWire runs on your device. It has no server of its own and no account with us. We don't collect, store, sell or share your information, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your settings, the briefs the app builds, its activity log, and any keys or sign-ins you add are stored only on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, in the app's private storage and the system Keychain. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app reads, and from whom

  • YouTube. To find new videos and songs from the channels you choose, the app asks the YouTube Data API for public information (channel feeds, video titles, lengths and view counts) using an API key belonging to the app. It does not read or change your YouTube account. When you ask the setup helpers to suggest channels or artists, the app also looks each suggested name up on YouTube to confirm it exists; those lookups contain only the public channel handle. Playing your picks opens the YouTube app or youtube.com. YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy apply to that use.
  • News. To write your briefs, the app reads Google News search feeds and the Google Alerts feeds you give it, and then the web pages of the articles it picks, where the site allows it. These requests go from your device straight to those sites, so they can see ordinary web-request information such as your IP address.

Optional: Google sign-in for email

Only if you turn on emailing a brief, you sign in with Google and allow one permission: send email on your behalf (Gmail "send"). The app uses it only to send the brief you asked for, to the addresses you listed, from your own Gmail. It cannot read, search or delete your mail. The sign-in token is kept in your device's Keychain and is sent only to Google. You can remove it any time with Sign Out in the app, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. EarlyWire's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Optional: Claude

If you add your own Anthropic API key, the app sends the following to Anthropic's Claude, and only when you use the feature that needs it:

  • Your briefs. The headlines, snippets and article text the app picked, so Claude can choose and summarize items.
  • Your requests to the setup helpers. When you use "Set it up for me", "Suggest Settings", "Suggest more keywords", "Find Suggestions" or "Adjust with words", the app sends the words you typed (for example, a description of your morning or a topic), along with what the request needs to make sense: your brief's name and keywords, the names of channels already on your list, and the current values of the settings you are adjusting.

Your key is stored in your Keychain and goes only to Anthropic, under Anthropic's privacy policy and your own agreement with them. We never see your key or your requests.

Without a key, nothing is sent to Anthropic. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the same helpers use Apple's on-device model, so what you type is processed on your device. Without either, the helpers are hidden and the app uses simple rules.

Notifications, widgets, Siri and Listen

Reminders, widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, and the read-aloud voice all work on your device. If you turn on voice commands for Listen, speech recognition happens on the device.

Your choices

  • Use the app with no Google sign-in and no Claude key; nothing is sent to us either way.
  • Sign out, remove a key, or delete the app at any time to remove what it holds.

Children

EarlyWire is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: support@earlywire.app.
