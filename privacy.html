Privacy policy

EarlyWire, by SMS Apps. Last updated October 18, 2026.

The short version

EarlyWire runs on your device. It has no server of its own and no account with us. We don't collect, store, sell or share your information, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your settings, the briefs the app builds, its activity log, and any keys or sign-ins you add are stored only on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, in the app's private storage and the system Keychain. Deleting the app deletes them. Your settings and latest builds can also be kept the same on your devices through iCloud, as described below.

iCloud: keeping your devices in step

If you are signed in to iCloud, EarlyWire keeps your devices in step by storing copies in your own iCloud account, using Apple's iCloud key-value storage and iCloud Drive (in a folder only the app uses). Apple holds them under your Apple Account and Apple's terms. They are not sent to us, and we cannot read them.

  • Your settings. Your channels and artists, rules, schedules, and your written briefs with their names, keywords, exceptions and any email recipients you entered.
  • Your latest builds. The most recent picks on Today (video and song titles, channels and links), the history of recent builds (what was built, when, and any notes), and the latest few builds of each written brief (headlines, short excerpts from articles, outlet names and links). This lets one device show what another already built, and avoids building the same morning twice.

Not included: your Google sign-in, which each device keeps for itself, and your API keys, which travel separately through iCloud Keychain (see below). You can turn syncing off under Settings, Schedule and Account, iCloud. Deleting the app does not remove the copies in iCloud; you can manage them in your device's iCloud storage settings.

What the app reads, and from whom

  • YouTube. To find new videos and songs from the channels you choose, the app reads YouTube's public channel feeds (video titles, times and view counts). It uses the YouTube Data API, with an API key belonging to the app, only for occasional lookups, such as finding a channel you add or the name of a playlist you follow. It does not read or change your YouTube account. When you ask the setup helpers to suggest channels or artists, the app also looks each suggested name up on YouTube to confirm it exists; those lookups contain only the public channel handle. If you add a YouTube API key of your own under Settings, Schedule and Account, the app also uses it to read video details (such as lengths and where a video can be watched); the key is stored in your Keychain and sent only to Google, with those requests; remove it there at any time. The app keeps video details it reads on your device and deletes anything older than 30 days. Playing your picks opens the YouTube app or youtube.com. YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy apply to that use.
  • News. To write your briefs, the app reads Google News search feeds and the Google Alerts feeds you give it, and then the web pages of the articles it picks, where the site allows it. These requests go from your device straight to those sites, so they can see ordinary web-request information such as your IP address.

Optional: Google sign-in for email

Only if you turn on emailing a brief, you sign in with Google and allow one permission: send email on your behalf (Gmail "send"). The app uses it only to send the brief you asked for, to the addresses you listed, from your own Gmail. It cannot read, search or delete your mail. The sign-in token is kept in your device's Keychain and is sent only to Google. You can remove it any time with Sign Out in the app, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. EarlyWire's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Optional: Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini

If you add your own key for Claude (made by Anthropic), ChatGPT (made by OpenAI) or Gemini (made by Google), the app sends the following to the one you chose, and only when you use the feature that needs it. One is used at a time.

  • Your briefs. The headlines, snippets and article text the app picked, so the AI can choose and summarize items.
  • Your requests to the setup helpers. When you use "Set it up for me", "Suggest Settings", "Suggest more keywords", "Find Suggestions" or "Describe a change", the app sends the words you typed (for example, a description of your morning or a topic), along with what the request needs to make sense: your brief's name and keywords, the names of channels already on your list, and the current values of the settings you are adjusting.

Your keys on all your devices. The API keys you add (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and your own YouTube key) are kept as synchronizable items in iCloud Keychain, which Apple encrypts end to end, so they work on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. They are never sent to us. On a device with iCloud Keychain turned off, a key stays on that device. Removing a key removes it from all your devices.

Your key is stored in your Keychain and goes only to the company that made that AI, under its privacy policy and your own agreement with it: Anthropic, OpenAI or Google. On Gemini's free plan, Google may use what is sent to improve its products; see Google's Gemini API terms. We never see your key or your requests.

Without a key, nothing is sent to Anthropic, OpenAI or Google's Gemini. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the same helpers use Apple's on-device model, so what you type is processed on your device. Without either, the helpers are hidden and the app uses simple rules.

Notifications, widgets, Siri and Listen

Reminders, widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, and the read-aloud voice all work on your device. If you turn on voice commands for Listen, speech recognition happens on the device. If you choose to have briefs read in your Personal Voice, EarlyWire asks your permission first, and the voice never leaves your device.

Your choices

  • Use the app with no Google sign-in and no AI key; nothing is sent to us either way.
  • Sign out, remove a key, or delete the app at any time to remove what it holds.

Children

EarlyWire is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
